------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BR-L1  Correction detection in code
  Rule:   A message is a correction of the last saved entry when it matches, in code:
          "no, I mean", "no na", "no e be", "no be", "I mean", "correction", "actually",
          "sorry, I mean / na / e be", "abeg change", "change am". It applies only when
          no clarification is pending and a last entry exists.
  Source: llm/index.py:CORRECTION_PATTERN, is_correction; ledger/index.py:_process_text
  PRD:    FR-S6
  Pre-check: VALIDATED as an offline / pre-filter signal. Our AI intent already returns
          "correction"; use this pattern for DEMO_MODE and as a guard.
  Task:   3.1
  STATUS: pending

BR-L2  A complete new entry abandons a pending question
  Rule:   While a clarification is pending, a message of 3+ words that is a complete
          entry on its own (has an item or a customer, nothing missing) deletes the
          pending entry and is processed as new. A "yes" never does this.
  Source: ledger/index.py:_is_fresh_entry
  PRD:    FR-S5 says "the next message completes it"; it does not cover a trader who moves on.
  Pre-check: DECISION (sensible extension; costs an extra extraction call per message).
  Task:   2.3
  STATUS: VALIDATED (2026-10-24, Phase 2). Decided: a different, complete entry (3+ words, not yes/no) replaces the pending one. Implemented in ledger/services.py:starts_over.

BR-L3  Clarifying questions that use what is already known
  Rule:   Missing amount with quantity and unit known:
            sale / credit sale -> "How much did you collect for the 2.5 bags?"
            expense            -> "How much did you pay for the 5 bags?"
          Missing amount otherwise -> "How much was it?"
          Missing customer for credit_sale / payment_received -> "Which customer is this for?"
          Units are pluralised when quantity > 1 (not "kg").
  Source: ledger/index.py:_missing_question
  PRD:    FR-S5 acceptance uses exactly "How much did you collect for the 2.5 bags?"
  Pre-check: VALIDATED. Replace our generic fallback questions in ledger/services.py.
  Task:   2.3
  STATUS: VALIDATED (2026-10-24, Phase 2). Implemented in ledger/services.py:missing_question (code wording is used for missing fields).

BR-L4  Low confidence
  Rule:   Confidence below 0.6 asks "I didn't fully understand. What did you sell or
          spend, and for how much?". When the trader answers our own question and
          supplies an amount, confidence is raised to at least 0.6.
  Source: ledger/index.py:_missing_question, _process_text
  PRD:    FR-S5 ("confidence is below a threshold")
  Pre-check: CONFLICT with our design: ours uses CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD from .env and asks
          a confirmation of what was heard. Keep ours; the "answering our own question"
          rule is worth reusing so a reply is not gated again.
  Task:   2.3
  STATUS: Threshold part CONFLICT (kept CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD and our confirmation question). 'Answering our own question is not gated again' VALIDATED and implemented (2026-10-24, Phase 2).

BR-L5  Customer resolution order
  Rule:   1. Alias match anywhere in the message text (word boundary, case-insensitive).
             - exactly one customer  -> link
             - several               -> pick the one whose canonical name equals the
                                         mention; otherwise ask "Do you mean A or B?"
          2. Canonical name equals the mention -> link
          3. Model matches the mention against the trader's customers and aliases:
             - confidence >= 0.85 -> link, and save the mention as a new alias
             - 0.5 to 0.85        -> ask "Do you mean <name>?" (only once the amount is known)
          4. Otherwise, with a mention -> new customer.
  Source: ledger/index.py:_resolve_customer, _alias_matches; llm/index.py:match_customer
  PRD:    FR-S9 ("alias table first, then the LLM against the existing customer list";
          "Unsure matches ask 'Do you mean Mama Ngozi?'")
  Pre-check: VALIDATED. Thresholds 0.85 / 0.5 should come from .env.
  Task:   2.3
  STATUS: VALIDATED (2026-10-24, Phase 2). Implemented in LedgerOrchestrator._resolve_customer; thresholds in .env (CUSTOMER_MATCH_LINK_CONFIDENCE, CUSTOMER_MATCH_CONFIRM_CONFIDENCE).

BR-L6  Yes / no answers to "Do you mean X?"
  Rule:   Yes  (yes, yeah, yep, correct, exactly, "na she / he / am / her / him",
               "e be so", ehen, ehn, ok) -> link X, save the mention as an alias,
               save the entry (confidence at least 0.8).
          No   (no, nope, "not her / him", "no be she / he / am") -> another single
               alias match in the reply, else a new customer from the mention, else
               ask "Which customer is this for?".
          Anything else -> repeat the same question; nothing is saved.
  Source: ledger/index.py:YES_PATTERN, NO_PATTERN, _process_text
  PRD:    FR-S9
  Pre-check: VALIDATED. Detection runs in code (not the model).
  Task:   2.3
  STATUS: VALIDATED (2026-10-24, Phase 2). Implemented in LedgerOrchestrator._answer_confirmation; a confirmed entry's confidence is raised to CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD (not a fixed 0.8). Also applied to 'Just to confirm ...?': no -> nothing saved.

BR-L9  Stock effect of an entry
  Rule:   - At most one stock movement per entry, updated in place on correction.
          - Units are normalised to singular for comparison ("bags" -> "bag"; "kg" unchanged).
          - If the entry gives no unit, it takes the inventory item's unit.
          - A unit that differs from the item's unit moves no stock.
          - Sale larger than stock on hand: NO movement, flag "check_stock".
          - Expense with item + quantity + unit and no inventory item: creates a new
            inventory item (unit cost = amount / quantity) and restocks it.
          - Sale of an item not in inventory: flag "not_in_inventory".
          - The flag is stored on the entry (stock_warning) and returned with it.
  Source: ledger/index.py:_sync_stock; llm/index.py:normalize_unit
  PRD:    FR-S25, FR-S26
  Pre-check: mixed.
          VALIDATED: one movement per entry; flags; unit default from the item.
          DECISION (resolved, D6): units compare with match_key; a different unit is flagged check_stock.
          CONFLICT: "sale larger than stock moves no stock". PRD FR-S25 says the sale
            "is still saved" and flagged; it does not say stock stays unchanged. Ours
            moves stock (can go negative). Agree one rule.
          CONFLICT: auto-creating an item from an expense. FR-S26 says "an expense with
            no matching item ... changes no stock".
          DECISION: storing the flag on the entry (useful for the dashboard, needs a
            column and migration).
  Task:   4.4 (units), 2.1 / 2.7 (flag storage)
  STATUS: Partly done (2026-10-24, Phase 2). Implemented: one movement per entry; unit defaults to the item's; flag stored on entries.stock_warning (decided, migration c3819b46ad50); oversell saves with NO movement (decided). CONFLICT, not ported: auto-creating an item from an expense. Pending D6: unit normalisation and mismatch.

BR-A2  Due dates are computed in code, never by the model
  Rule:   "tomorrow" / "tmrw" -> +1 day; "next tomorrow" -> +2; "next week" -> +7;
          a weekday name -> its next occurrence (the same weekday as today -> +7).
          Only for credit sales. Relative to the trader's current date.
  Source: llm/index.py:_due_date, extract_entry
  PRD:    FR-S12 ("Relative dates such as 'Friday' resolve to a concrete date using the
          trader's current date")
  Pre-check: VALIDATED. Use Lagos date (app.shared.periods.LAGOS), not UTC.
  Task:   2.5

BR-A3  Rule-based offline extractor
  Rule:   Used when DEMO_MODE is on or no provider is reachable.
          Type keywords:
            credit_sale      "on credit", "owe", "go pay", "put am for account", "will pay",
                             "credit", "pay later", "pay me later", "account"
            payment_received "paid me", "don pay", "pay me", "received payment",
                             "pay back", "paid"  (only when not a credit phrase)
            expense          "i spend", "i spent", "expense", "transport", "i buy",
                             "i bought", "go buy", "went buy", "i don buy", "restock",
                             "i purchase", "i purchased"
            otherwise sale
          Quantity: number or number word + unit, with "and (a) half" adding 0.5.
            Units: bag, bottle, measure, derica, painter, piece, tin, kg, litre, carton,
            basket, packet, pack, crate, sachet, keg, bundle, dozen, tuber, paint, roll.
          Amount: last number >= 50 outside the quantity phrase.
          Items: palm oil, vegetable oil, groundnut oil, tomatoes, garri, beans, rice,
            yam, oil; else the word after "<qty> <unit> (of)". Customer nicknames are
            removed from the text first, so "the rice woman" is not the item.
          Customer: a known name or alias; else "<name> don pay / paid me / pay me",
            "on <name>'s account", or a titled name ("Mama X", "Mr X", "Iya X", ...).
            Several known customers mentioned -> leave empty so the trader is asked.
          Confidence: 0.85 when complete, 0.45 otherwise.
          With a pending entry: fills the missing amount and/or customer from the reply.
  Source: llm/index.py:_demo_extract, _quantity, _amount, _match_known_customer
  PRD:    NFR-4 (DEMO_MODE), golden path offline
  Pre-check: VALIDATED for DEMO_MODE. Port it to produce our Interpretation shape, with
          the amount as amount_text so parse_naira_amount still decides the figure.
  Task:   3.1
  STATUS: pending

BR-O3  Phone + PIN accounts
  Rule:   PIN of 4 to 6 digits, stored as scrypt (n=2^14, r=8, p=1, 16-byte salt).
          Login: wrong phone and wrong PIN give the same error and take the same time
          (a dummy hash is checked for unknown phones). 5 wrong PINs lock the account
          for 5 minutes (429 "account_locked"). Logout revokes only this device's
          session. A PIN is optional at onboarding and can be set later via
          /auth/change-pin; login works only after a PIN exists. Change PIN requires
          the current PIN once one exists. There is no separate /auth/signup endpoint.
  PRD:    not in the PRD; D7 says "No OTP or password".
  Decision: D1 resolved (2026-10-24): port PIN login; PIN is optional at onboarding or
          can be set later by an authenticated trader through /auth/change-pin.
  Task:   3.5
  STATUS: VALIDATED (2026-10-24 decision). Implemented in onboarding schemas, services,
          orchestrator and router; see the auth tests.

BR-O4  Session expiry
  Rule:   Each session expires after SESSION_TOKEN_TTL_DAYS; expired or revoked -> 401.
          Legacy sessions with expires_at=NULL receive a persisted expiry of
          created_at + SESSION_TOKEN_TTL_DAYS on first authentication. If that computed
          expiry has passed, reject the token with 401.
  PRD:    NFR-7, FR-C5
  Decision: preserve legacy sessions only until their original configured lifetime ends;
          never treat a missing expiry as permanent.
  Task:   3.5
  STATUS: VALIDATED and implemented (2026-10-24); legacy expiry is assigned and checked
          in OnboardingOrchestrator.authenticate_session.
