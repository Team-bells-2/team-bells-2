OJA backend: open items to resolve with the team
================================================

1. Item name matching (inventory, ledger and suggestions)

   Problem:
   Items are matched by exact name, ignoring only case and surrounding spaces.
   - Ledger -> inventory (FR-S25, FR-S26): a sale or expense only moves stock when its item
     name equals an inventory item name. "rice" matches "Rice", but "bag of rice",
     "foreign rice" or "rices" do not, so the entry is saved with no stock change and
     flagged "not_in_inventory".
   - Inventory -> suggestions (FR-S30): GET /suggestions?item= looks up supplier offers by
     the same exact name. A trader item called "palm oil" finds nothing, because the seeded
     offers use "oil".
   - Units are not compared at all: a sale of "2 bags" moves stock on an item kept in
     "bag", and a sale in "cups" would move it too.
   The PRD says matching uses the item name first, then the LLM against the trader's
   inventory list (same approach as customer aliases, FR-S9). Only the first half exists.

   Options to discuss:
   a. Item aliases table (like customer_aliases), filled at onboarding and by the trader.
   b. LLM fallback: when no exact match, ask the model to pick from the trader's inventory
      list, and ask "Do you mean Rice?" when unsure (as the PRD describes).
   c. A shared canonical item list (rice, beans, garri, oil) that inventory items and
      supplier offers both point to.
   d. Unit handling: normalise singular/plural units, or flag a unit mismatch instead of
      moving stock.

   Code: InventoryRepository.get_item_by_name, SupplierRepository.list_offers_for_item,
   LedgerOrchestrator._apply_stock_effect.
